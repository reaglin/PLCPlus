# Relays and contactors

A **relay** is a switch worked by an electromagnet (its **coil**). Energize the coil and all its **contacts** change over.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Coil | The electromagnet. *Energized* = has voltage across it |
| Normally open (NO) | Open while the coil is off; closes when it is energized |
| Normally closed (NC) | Closed while the coil is off; opens when it is energized |
| Contactor | A heavy-duty relay that switches a motor's current |
| Auxiliary contact | A small extra contact on a contactor, used in the control circuit (the seal-in uses one) |
