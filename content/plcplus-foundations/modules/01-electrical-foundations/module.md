A PLC is a computer that switches electricity. Its inputs read voltages from buttons and sensors, and its outputs turn on lights, relay coils and motor contactors. To program one well, and to find out why a machine has stopped, you need to know what that electricity is doing.

This module gives you the words and the few rules that the rest of PLC+ uses:

1. **Voltage, current and resistance**, and Ohm's law, which links them.
2. **Series and parallel circuits**, and how switches wired in series and in parallel become the AND and OR of ladder logic.
3. **AC and DC**: which kind of power each part of a control panel uses, and why.
4. **Working safely**: the hazards in a panel and the lockout/tagout steps that keep you safe while you work on a machine.

Each item has a Learn page and a few practice questions on its **Exercise** tab. The module check at the end asks questions on all four. No earlier module is needed: start here.
