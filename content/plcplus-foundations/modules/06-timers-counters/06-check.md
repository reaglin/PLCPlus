# Module check: timers and counters

This check covers the five exercises of module 6: the traffic light, the exhaust fan, the pump's service light, the batch counter and the case packer. Each is complete when its program passes every test on the Exercise tab.

The check itself is ten questions, on the **Exercise** tab. Several ask **when** something happens: picture the timer's IN, ET and Q against time, as in the tables on the Learn pages, and type the answer in seconds. Two are **find the fault**: a machine from this module's Example tabs runs its working program, but one of its devices or wires is faulty. Work the machine, watch the timers and counters on the rungs, and say what is wrong. You need 80 % to pass, and you can try again.

When all five exercises pass and you have passed the questions, the module is complete and ready to hand in: **Hand in a report** (the second row of the path) makes the PDF progress report for your instructor, with your score on each exercise and a picture of each program.
