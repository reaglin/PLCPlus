# Interlocks and limit switches

- **Interlock:** each of two outputs that must never be on together has a normally closed contact of the other in its rung. Whichever starts first locks the other out.
- **Limit switch:** a switch worked by the moving part at the end of its travel. A normally closed contact of it in the direction's rung stops the motion at the limit.
- In a real panel the interlock is also wired between the contactors, and often mechanical too, because a welded contact could defeat the program.
