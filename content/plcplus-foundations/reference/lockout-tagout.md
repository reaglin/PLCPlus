# Lockout/tagout

**An introduction only. It does not replace the safety training your lab or employer gives you, and it does not qualify you to work on live equipment.** Follow their written procedure every time.

**The rules:** OSHA 29 CFR 1910.147, *The control of hazardous energy (lockout/tagout)*; NFPA 70E, *Standard for Electrical Safety in the Workplace*.

1. **Prepare and notify:** know every energy source; tell everyone affected.
2. **Shut down** with the machine's normal controls.
3. **Isolate** every energy source (disconnect, valves).
4. **Apply your own lock and tag.** You keep the only key.
5. **Release or block stored energy:** air, hydraulics, springs, raised parts, capacitors.
6. **Verify zero energy:** try the Start with everyone clear, then return it to off; test for voltage **live-dead-live** (meter on a known live source, the circuit, the live source again).

**Not a lockout:** an emergency stop, a stopped PLC program, a pressed Stop button, or a tag with no lock where a lock fits.

**Live work:** only if you are qualified, trained for the task, permitted, and wearing the protective equipment it calls for.
