# Module check: logic and number systems

This check covers the four items of module 2. Each of them is complete once you have passed its practice questions on its **Exercise** tab.

The check itself is ten questions, on the **Exercise** tab: numbers to write in binary, hex and BCD, numbers to read back, choices, a match and a truth table. You need 80 % to pass, and you can try again as often as you like. A right answer says why; a wrong one tells you to look again, not what the answer is.

Keep the Reference tab of each item at hand: the 0–15 table, the BCD digits and the logic tables are there. Work on paper if it helps. For a conversion, do it twice, once each way, to check yourself.

When the four items are complete and you have passed the questions, the module is complete and ready to hand in: **Hand in a report** (the second row of the path) makes the PDF progress report for your instructor.
