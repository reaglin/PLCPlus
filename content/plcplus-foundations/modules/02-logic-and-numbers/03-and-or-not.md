# AND, OR and NOT

*This page is an early sample of a Logic and number systems lesson, here to show the truth-table widget.*

A logic signal is either **TRUE** (on, 1) or **FALSE** (off, 0). Three operations combine them:

- **AND** is TRUE only when both inputs are TRUE.
- **OR** is TRUE when either input is TRUE, or both.
- **NOT** turns TRUE into FALSE and FALSE into TRUE.

A **truth table** lists every combination of the inputs and the result for each. With two inputs there are four rows.
