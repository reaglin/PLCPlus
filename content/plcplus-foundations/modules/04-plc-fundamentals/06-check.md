# Module check: PLC fundamentals

This check covers the five items of module 4. Each of them is complete when you pass its practice questions on its Exercise tab.

The check itself is ten questions, on the **Exercise** tab: choices, matching, numbers, a typed address, and two **find the fault** questions. In those, a machine runs its working program, but one of its inputs has a wiring fault. Work the machine, watch the rungs and the wiring list, and say what is wrong. You need 80 % to pass, and you can try again as often as you like. A right answer says why; a wrong one tells you to look again, not what the answer is.

When the five items are complete and you have passed the questions, the module is complete and ready to hand in: **Hand in a report** (the second row of the path) makes the PDF progress report for your instructor.
