# Module check: sequencing and state machines

This check covers the four items of module 8. The stamping station, the batch tank and the gate are complete when their programs pass every test on the Exercise tab; the SFC item is complete when its practice questions are passed.

The check itself is ten questions, on the **Exercise** tab. Some ask **what step or state** a machine is in from what you can see. Three are **find the fault**: a machine runs the working program, but a sensor's wire is broken or a solenoid has failed. Work the machine, watch the rungs and the cylinders, and say what is wrong. A failed solenoid looks different from a broken sensor: in one, the rung turns the output on and nothing moves; in the other, the cylinder arrives and the program never notices. You need 80 % to pass, and you can try again.

When all the items are complete and you have passed the questions, the module is complete and ready to hand in: **Hand in a report** (the second row of the path) makes the PDF progress report for your instructor, with your score on each exercise and a picture of each program.
