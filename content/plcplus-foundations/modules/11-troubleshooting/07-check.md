# Module check: troubleshooting

This check covers the six items of module 11: the three readings (each complete when its practice questions are passed) and the three programs you fixed (each complete when it passes every test on its Exercise tab).

The check itself is ten questions, on the **Exercise** tab. Four of them are **find the fault**: a machine runs its working program, but something on it is faulty, on the input side or the output side. Use the method:

1. work the machine as the question says, and see the symptom;
2. look at the outputs: is the coil TRUE while the device does nothing?
3. look at the inputs: does each contact change when it should, and only then?
4. compare the rungs with **What every part is doing**, and use a force to be sure.

The other questions are about the method and the three program bugs. You need 80 % to pass, and you can try again: a wrong answer says where to look again.

When all six items are complete and you have passed the questions, the module is complete and ready to hand in: **Hand in a report** (the second row of the path) makes the PDF progress report for your instructor, with your score on each exercise and a picture of each program you fixed.
