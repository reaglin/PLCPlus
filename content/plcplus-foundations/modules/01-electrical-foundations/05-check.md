# Module check: electrical foundations

This check covers the four items of module 1. Each of them is complete once you have passed its practice questions on its **Exercise** tab.

The check itself is ten questions, on the **Exercise** tab: choices, numbers worked out with Ohm's law, matching the parts of a panel to their power, and a truth table. You need 80 % to pass, and you can try again as often as you like. A right answer says why; a wrong one tells you to look again, not what the answer is. Keep a calculator to hand.

When the four items are complete and you have passed the questions, the module is complete and ready to hand in: **Hand in a report** (the second row of the path) makes the PDF progress report for your instructor.
