# Ohm's law and power

| Quantity | Symbol | Unit | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| Voltage | V | volt (V) | The push, measured between two points |
| Current | I | ampere (A) | The flow through the circuit |
| Resistance | R | ohm (Ω) | How hard a part makes it for current to pass |
| Power | P | watt (W) | How fast energy is used |

**Ohm's law:** V = I × R, so I = V / R and R = V / I.

**Power:** P = V × I.

| Prefix | Means | Example |
|---|---|---|
| m (milli) | ÷ 1000 | 50 mA = 0.05 A |
| k (kilo) | × 1000 | 1.2 kΩ = 1200 Ω |
| M (mega) | × 1 000 000 | 1 MΩ = 1 000 000 Ω |

Change to V, A and Ω before you calculate. Example: a 24 V DC coil of 480 Ω draws 24 V / 480 Ω = 0.05 A = 50 mA, and uses 24 V × 0.05 A = 1.2 W.

**Open circuit:** a break in the path; no current. A PLC input with a broken wire reads FALSE all the time.

**Short circuit:** a path with almost no resistance; a very large current until a fuse or breaker opens. A shorted switch makes its input read TRUE all the time.
